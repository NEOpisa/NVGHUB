## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment:** This is the textbook volt-yellow CrossFit template — `#0A0A0A` + `#E8FF00` + Barlow Condensed 900 italic caps. It is exactly the first-order category reflex: anyone can name the genre and palette from "CrossFit box" alone in under a second. It's competently built, but it is the category cliché PRODUCT.md lists as anti-reference #1. Secondary tells: alternating section backgrounds (`#0A0A0A → #141414 → #1E1E1E`) creating visual noise instead of unity, clip-path polygon CTAs (2015-era edge), Unsplash hero hardcoded in CSS.

**Deterministic scan:** 2 warnings. (1) `side-tab` at globals.css:359 — **false positive**, it's a 1px neutral grid divider between dashboard cells, not an accent stripe. (2) `layout-transition` at globals.css:373 — real but minor; the progress bar animates `width`, swap to `transform:scaleX`.

## Overall Impression

Structurally strong, aesthetically anonymous. The build quality is real — a full landing page with a member dashboard and owner dashboard, responsive collapse, reduced-motion support. But it screams "AI/template CrossFit site," and the pricing cards show **`R$ 0,00`**, which on a conversion-first page is a credibility hole at the exact moment of decision. The single biggest opportunity is the redesign already specced in DESIGN.md (forge orange, DM Sans/Mono, micro-borders, product-as-showcase) — it directly fixes the category-reflex problem.

## What's Working

1. **The two dashboards (AreaAluno + owner Dashboard).** Genuine product surfaces with KPIs, charts, alerts — real proof of technical credibility. PRODUCT.md is right to make these the showcase.
2. **Disciplined responsive collapse.** Clean breakpoints at 900/600px; grids fold to single column, hero stats reflow, nav goes to hamburger. No overflow observed.
3. **Reduced-motion respected** globally (`prefers-reduced-motion` kills all animation).

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Pricing shows `R$ 0,00` on every plan.** On a conversion-first page this reads as broken, not "free." It undermines the one job the page has. **Fix:** populate real prices in the Planos data source. **Command:** `/impeccable clarify`
- **[P1] Category-reflex aesthetic (volt-yellow CrossFit cliché).** Fails the AI-slop test at first order; matches PRODUCT.md anti-reference #1 verbatim. **Fix:** execute the DESIGN.md redesign — forge orange accent, DM Sans body / DM Mono data, kill alternating backgrounds for 1px micro-borders + vertical rhythm. **Command:** `/impeccable craft`
- **[P2] Alternating section backgrounds.** `#0A0A0A/#141414/#1E1E1E` ping-pong creates noise and breaks unity. **Fix:** one base bg, separate sections with `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.06)` + spacing. **Command:** `/impeccable layout`
- **[P2] Unsplash hero hardcoded in CSS.** External CDN dependency, no brand identity, fragile. **Fix:** replace with CSS radial-gradient forge-glow per DESIGN.md. **Command:** `/impeccable craft`
- **[P2] clip-path polygon CTAs.** Applied to `.nav-aula`, `.btn-volt`, `.porque-badge`, `.plano-badge-top` — dated, inconsistent. **Fix:** 2-3px radius, no clip-path. **Command:** `/impeccable craft`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer):** Lands on `R$ 0,00` pricing — is it free or broken? Hesitates, distrusts. The `[Nome da Academia]` placeholder in nav/footer signals "unfinished site." Will question legitimacy.

**Riley (Stress Tester):** The experimental booking form has no visible validation — submits empty/garbage and gets no inline error (heuristic #9 = 2). Owner dashboard `period-select` changes label but data is static mock; a probing user notices it does nothing.

**Casey (Mobile):** Largely fine — hero stats reflow, nav collapses to hamburger. But the hero headline `clamp(3.5rem,20vw,6rem)` at 20vw is aggressive on small phones; check the longest word doesn't kiss the edges. Schedule table forces horizontal scroll (min-width 700px) — acceptable but thumb-unfriendly.

## Minor Observations

- `.prog-bar-fill` animates `width` (detector flag) — use `transform:scaleX` for GPU compositing.
- Hero `line3` uses `-webkit-text-stroke` outline text — fragile across browsers, low contrast by design.
- `--muted: #888` on `#0A0A0A` is ~5.4:1 (passes), but on `#1E1E1E` surfaces it drops toward the AA floor — verify per-surface.

## Questions to Consider

- The two dashboards are the strongest asset but sit near the bottom (positions 11–12 of 14). What if the member dashboard moved up to right after Porque, as the DESIGN.md showcase move proposes?
- The page has 14 sections. Does a conversion-first landing page need all of them above the booking CTA, or is Experimental buried too deep?
- What would a version that's unmistakably *this* box — not "a CrossFit box" — look like?
