brand

## Users

**Primary:** Small local business owners — barbearias, restaurantes, lojas, clínicas. Age 30–55, non-technical. The decision maker is evaluating whether NeoVanguard is serious enough to trust and accessible enough to approach. They're not reading about tech; they're asking "will this actually help my business?"

**Secondary:** Potential partners, freelancers, and future investors who want to assess the team's competence and professionalism.

## Product Purpose

NeoVanguard is a four-person digital agency (Mizael, Daniel, Kauã, João) in early-stage formation, targeting small local businesses that lack any digital presence. The site's job is to build enough credibility to convert the first paying clients — before the company has a formal CNPJ, track record, or marketing budget.

Success = a business owner lands on the page, immediately understands what NeoVanguard delivers, trusts the team is real and competent, and takes the first step (WhatsApp or form submission).

## Brand Personality

**Confiável · Direto · Acessível**

Serious enough to generate trust; human enough not to intimidate. Language is concrete and result-oriented: "seu site em até 7 dias" beats "soluções inovadoras" every time. No jargon, no buzzwords, no chest-puffing. The brand sounds like a skilled friend who knows the craft and explains it plainly.

## Anti-references

- Agency/portfolio sites with excessive animation, creative chaos, or "we're artists" energy
- Buzzwords: "transformação digital", "cutting-edge", "infraestrutura", "stack", "deploy" (in client-facing copy)
- Glassmorphism used decoratively
- Gradient text
- Cards in identical grid layouts (rigid, soulless)
- Eyebrow labels on every section
- Cream/sand backgrounds
- Generic stock photography

## Design Principles

1. **Resultado primeiro, feature depois.** Every claim is written from the client's outcome, not the agency's capability. "Você aparece no Google" > "site com SEO otimizado."
2. **Confiança pela clareza.** If a visitor has to pause to understand what NeoVanguard does or costs, the page failed. Clarity is the premium.
3. **Seriedade sem distância.** The visual weight says "competent professionals"; the copy says "we're approachable humans." Neither softens the other.
4. **Densidade controlada.** Generous whitespace, weighted typography, deliberate density — not sparse minimalism, not information overload. Linear/Railway/Resend as tone references.
5. **Momentum without noise.** Motion is purposeful (entrance on scroll, hover feedback on CTAs) and never decorative. If removing an animation makes the page feel more trustworthy, remove it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target: WCAG 2.1 AA.

- Contrast ratios ≥ 4.5:1 for body text, ≥ 3:1 for large text and UI components
- Full keyboard navigation; visible focus indicators
- Screen reader semantics (landmarks, proper heading hierarchy, alt text)
- Reduced-motion support via `prefers-reduced-motion` for all scroll and hover animations
- No color-only information encoding
